# operations 

[[union of a family]] $$\bigcup_{i \in I} M_i : = \{ x| \exists i \in I ; x \in M_i \}$$
[[intersection of a family]]
$$\bigcap_{i \in I} M_i : = \{ x | \forall i \in I : x \in M_i \}$$
