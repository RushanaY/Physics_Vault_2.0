## nucleus mass, charge and mass number

- mass of the nucleus$$M_k = Z \cdot m_p  N \cdot m_n - B/c^2$$
	- it is the mass with the added defect and other corrections (like $\langle \frac{B}{A} \rangle \approx 8MeV$ )
	- to be very explicit: [[Mattausches Massenspektrometer]] $$\frac{M_k}{Q} = \frac{|\vec B|^2 r_B^2}{|\vec E| r_E}$$
- charge of the [[nucleus]] 
	- using the characteristic x rays of the $K_{\alpha}$  
	- according to the [[Mosley law]]
- mass number $$A \approx \frac{M_k}{\frac{1}{2} (m_p + m_n) - \langle \frac{B}{A} \rangle \frac{1}{c^2}}$$
- [[unifed atomic mass/Dalton]] $$1u = \frac{1}{12}M_{12C} = \frac{1g}{N_A}  = 1.66 \cdot 10^{-17}kg = 931.484 \space MeV/c^2$$
	- typical bining energy of $7-8 MeV$ per [[nucleus]]
- the frequency of elements
	- from [[Big bang nycleosynthesis]]/ primordialer Nukleosynthese 
	- from the fusion of the stars 
	- from star explosions ([[supernova]])

## [[magnetic moment]]
- [[angular momentum]] $\vec J$ and [[magnetic field]] $\vec B$ make a system 
	=> General angular momentum $$\vec J = \vec L + \vec S$$
		- with $\vec L$ being the [[angular momentum]] and $\vec S$ the [[spin]] 
	- it comes to energy level splitting, which defined though the [[Lande factor]]
	- $$E_{mag} = - \vec \mu \vec B$$
	- $\vec \mu=  g_k \mu_k \frac{\vec J}{\hbar}$
	- $\mu_K = \frac{e \hbar}{2m_p} = 3.10 \times 10^{-8} \frac{eV}{T}$
	- $\mu_B = \frac{e \hbar}{2 m_e} = 5.79 \times 10^{-5} \frac{eV}{T}$ 
	- $g=2$ for point particlse without any corrections and $g \neq 2$ for anomalymagnetic moments
- measurement of the magnetic moment
	- classicaly ([[Rabi]] 1939) 
		- in the big blocks we have $F_Z = \mu_z \frac{\partial B}{\partial z}$ 
		- in the middle, where $B>0$ ->  $\Delta E = \Delta \mu_z B$ 
	- [[nuclear magnetic resonanse]] 
		- splitting of energy level in the B field $\Delta E = \Delta \mu_z B = g_K \mu_K \Delta m_i B$ 
		- Dipol transition with $\Delta m_i = 1$: $\Delta E = g_K \mu_K B = \hbar \omega_c \to \omega_L = \frac{1}{\hbar} g_K \mu_K B$
		- [[Lamour frequency]]

## Binding energy 
- experimentally:
	-  $B = (Z \cdot m_p + (A-Z)m_n - M_K)\cdot c^2$ from the nucleus reaction 
	- thermal [[neutron]]: $$E_n^{kin} = \frac{1}{40}eV$$
	- from [[conservation of energy]], [[conservation of momentum]] we get $$B = (m_n + M_{1H} - M_D) = E_{\gamma} + \frac{1}{2} \frac{E^2_{\gamma}}{M_D} = 2.225 MeV$$ 
[[Tröpfchenmodell/liquid drop modell]] 
- in [[nucleus]] - droplets out of condensed protones and neutrons 
- nucleus radius is $r = r_0 \sqrt[3]{A}, \space r_0 = 1.2fm$
- contribution from the [[binding energy]]
	- Volume energy or condensation energy $B \sim \text{Volumen} \sim r^3$ => $B_V = + q_VA$
	- Surface energy $B_0 \sim \text{Surface} \sim r^2$ => $B_0 - q_0 A^{\frac{2}{3}}$ 
	- [[Coulomb energy]] $B_C \sim \frac{Z(Z-1)}{r} \approx \frac{Z^2}{r}$ => $B_C = - a_c \frac{Z(Z-1)}{A^{1/3}}$ 
	- Asymmetric energy (related to the [[Fermi gas energy]]) => $B_A = - a_a \frac{(N-Z)^2}{A}$ 
	- Paarungsenergie $B_p = -a_p \frac{\delta}{A^{1/2}}$ with $\delta  = -1 (gg), 0 (ug, gu), 1 (uu)$ 
- [[Weizsächer Massenformel]] $$B = + a_V A - a_0 A^{2/3} - a_c \frac{Z(Z-1)}{A^{1/3}} - a_a \frac{(N-Z)^2}{A} - a_p \frac{\delta}{A^{1/2}}$$
	- having measured and fitted the curve we get the values:
		- $a_V = 19.67 MeV$
		- $a_0 = 17.23 MeV$ 
		- $a_c = 0.71 MeV$ 
		- $a_A = 23.29MeV$ 
		- $a_p = 12.2 MeV$
