# charge carriers 

- charge carriers $\neq$ charges 
- for metals: $$n = \frac{N_A Z \rho_m}{m_a}$$
	- $N_A$  Avogardo number 
	- Z 
	- $\rho_m$  mass density
	- $m_a$ atomic mass (is in u)
- velocity of charge carriers in a wire: $$I = v \cdot A \cdot n_e$$
	- $I$ current 
	- $v$ charge carrier velocity
	- $A$ Querschnitt
	- $n_e$ charge carrier density
