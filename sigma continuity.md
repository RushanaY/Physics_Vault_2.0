# Equalities 
For a [[measurable space]] and a [[measure]] 
- with $\mu ( \emptyset) =0$ it is equivalent:
	- $\mu$ is a measure 
	- $\mu$ is additive and $\sigma$ continous from underneath
- with $\mu(X) < \infty$ 
	- $\mu$ additive and $\sigma$ continous from above 
	- $\mu$ is additive and $\emptyset$ - continous
